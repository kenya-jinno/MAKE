Review of main_paper_MAKE37.pdf
Target journal: MDPI Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction (MAKE)
Reviewer: Codex
Date: 2026-07-09

Overall recommendation: minor revision

Summary
-------
Revision 37 substantially addresses the remaining issues from the previous
round. The manuscript now presents the method as a conditional diagnostic
workflow rather than a general bottleneck-selection solution. The torus
active-unit inconsistency is explicitly investigated, the V1/V2 rule is
properly described as a reporting convention rather than a statistical test,
the AE range is renamed a default search window, and the theory section now
states the compactness, surrogate, and AU-bridging assumptions more clearly.

The paper is now suitable in substance for publication after minor cleanup.
The remaining issues are not fundamental, but they affect reproducibility and
precision: the exact definition of the verification point still depends on
which Step-1 estimate is used, the torus margin is described inconsistently
as m >= 5 in the body and m >= 2 d_emb in the abstract/conclusion, and a few
global statements about multi-seed evidence overstate what is actually
multi-seed.

Assessment of previous review responses
---------------------------------------
The previous major comment 1 on the torus AU inconsistency has been
addressed well. Section 4.2 now states that AU = 2 at m = 3,4 is genuine
over-pruning, not a reporting error, and explains that the MSE cross-check
detects the topological obstruction. Table 4 and the surrounding text no
longer claim sharp torus saturation at m = 4.

The previous major comment 2 on V1/V2 thresholds has been mostly addressed.
Section 3.2.3 now pre-specifies m_ver as the smallest tested grid point
above 2 d_hat_ID, states that V1/V2 is not a validated statistical test, and
reports threshold-invariance and per-seed verdict information. One remaining
ambiguity is whether the threshold-invariance range is evaluated on
multi-seed means only or on per-seed verdicts.

The previous major comment 3 on over-presenting [d_hat_ID, 2 d_hat_ID] has
been addressed. Section 3.2.2 now calls it a default search window and
states that topology, noise, mixtures, and fidelity requirements may require
larger margins.

The previous major comment 4 on the water-filling proposition has been
addressed. Proposition 2 and Remark 2 now clearly distinguish positive rate
in the surrogate from empirical posterior-mean-variance AU and state that
nonlinear AU counts are not predicted quantitatively.

The previous major comment 5 on Step-1 validity has been addressed. Section
4.3.2 and Section 5.2 now distinguish stability from unbiasedness, explain
the raw-space DANCo degeneration, and describe the reference-AE route as
pragmatic rather than uniquely validated.

The previous major comment 6 on empirical scope has been addressed in tone.
The abstract, Table 3, Section 4.7, and Section 5.4 now clearly label
Fashion-MNIST Step 3, high-beta Conv-VAE recovery, and natural-image plateau
evidence as exploratory or unresolved. Some global wording still needs
tightening, as noted below.

The previous major comment 7 on the topological lower-bound proposition has
been addressed. Proposition 1 now assumes compactness, continuity, and exact
identity reconstruction, and the following paragraph correctly limits the
result to an idealized necessary condition.

Most previous minor comments were also addressed: the abstract is less
overstated, the torus row is marked as over-pruned, the verification point is
defined, search-cost accounting now includes symmetric Step-1 accounting,
DANCo = 784 is explained, PCA is treated as illustrative, kappa is handled
with caution, and supplementary-material expectations are stated.

Major comments
--------------
1. No remaining major methodological objection.

The central scientific issues from the previous review have been resolved or
appropriately reframed. The remaining points below are minor revisions needed
for precision, consistency, and final presentation rather than major
conceptual changes.

Minor comments
--------------
1. The verification point m_ver is still ambiguous when TwoNN and MLE give a
range for d_hat_ID.

Section 3.2.3 defines m_ver as the smallest tested grid point satisfying
m_ver >= 2 d_hat_ID. In Section 4.3.1, however, MNIST is summarized as
d_hat_ID approximately 10-12, with TwoNN near 10 and MLE near 11.3-11.9.
Section 4.3.4 then uses m_ver = 20 as the smallest tested point above
2 d_hat_ID. This is true if d_hat_ID = 10, but not if the Step-1 reference is
12, in which case 2 d_hat_ID is 24 and the smallest grid point in Table 6
above it is 32. The verdict is not affected, because m = 32 also passes, but
the protocol should specify whether m_ver is based on TwoNN, MLE, their
average, a conservative upper estimate, or a pre-registered primary
estimator.

2. The torus margin is described inconsistently.

Section 4.2 reports that the torus activates the third coordinate at m = 5
and remains at AU = 3 for m >= 5. The abstract and conclusion instead state
the cleaner but different condition "m >= 2 d_emb", i.e. m >= 6, and describe
boundary over-pruning below 2 d_emb. This is conservative, but it makes m = 5
fall into the "below 2 d_emb" region even though it succeeds. Please align
the wording, for example by saying "m >= 5 in the fine-grid experiment; in
the coarser reported grid, m = 6 = 2 d_emb is the first verified safe
point."

3. The statement that all main-text results are multi-seed is too broad.

Section 4.1 says that all main-text results use at least 3 seeds except
where stated. Several important main-text quantities are indeed explicitly
single-seed: Table 5 Step-1 AE sweep, Table 8 AE downstream results, and the
single-seed reference-AE latents re-analyzed in Table 6. Because these are
not hidden, this is not a serious flaw, but the global sentence should be
softened to "most main-text training sweeps" or "all main-text VAE
verification results, except exploratory cases."

4. The V1 threshold-sensitivity statement needs one clarification.

Section 3.2.3 states that verdicts are invariant for V1 thresholds in
(0.67, 0.94) and that per-seed verdicts are unanimous in every multi-seed
setting. For MNIST at m = 20, the mean activation ratio is 13.3/20 = 0.665,
very close to the lower edge. If one seed has AU = 14, its ratio is 0.70 and
would not pass at a threshold of 0.67. Please clarify whether the sensitivity
range applies only to the multi-seed mean verdict, while the per-seed
unanimity statement applies only to the default V1 = 0.9 threshold.

5. The Fashion-MNIST Step-1 value should be made numerically consistent.

Section 4.3.6 says Fashion-MNIST gives d_hat_ID approximately 9, then says
TwoNN converges to 9.9-10.1 for m_ref = 5 d_hat_ID to 10 d_hat_ID. If the
stable estimate is about 10, the text should not summarize it as
approximately 9 unless that value comes from a different estimator or an
earlier reference point. This matters because the protocol's windows depend
directly on the numerical estimate.

6. The search-cost comparison is now appropriately scoped, but the VAE path
depends on the same unresolved d_hat_ID convention.

Section 4.4 counts the VAE path as Step 1 plus only m = 20. If the
pre-specified d_hat_ID were taken as 12 rather than 10, the VAE verification
point would be m = 32, increasing the cost if both m = 20 and m = 32 are
trained or changing the selected point if only one is trained. This should be
made consistent with the primary-estimator rule requested in comment 1.

7. The claim that "all experiments follow these reporting requirements" is
slightly stronger than the evidence shown.

Section 3.2.3 requires reporting the number of seeds and inter-seed variance
of d_hat_ID and AU, m-grid and m_ref values, beta, delta, preprocessing, and
both TwoNN and MLE. The main text does not always show MLE or inter-seed
variance for every experiment, especially the boundary and exploratory
experiments. If the supplementary material contains these, the sentence
should say so explicitly; otherwise, soften the claim to "the main
experiments report these quantities where applicable, with full details in
the supplement."

8. Proposition 3 remains a sketch and should not be leaned on too strongly.

The statement in Proposition 3 is appropriately labeled a sketch, but the
connection between trustworthiness, neighbor-rank preservation, and a bound
on the TwoNN ratio distribution is still informal. The manuscript mostly
uses it as intuition, which is acceptable. Avoid presenting it as a rigorous
stability theorem in the abstract, conclusion, or future responses.

Typographical and presentation issues
-------------------------------------
1. MDPI production placeholders remain: empty received/revised/accepted
dates, article number 0, no final repository URL/DOI, and commented ORCID
placeholders. These should be resolved before final submission.

2. In Table 3, "Conv-VAE on MNIST" says beta strengthening restores V1 only,
while the abstract says "beta-strengthening restores it." To avoid ambiguity,
state "restores V1 but not V2" wherever this result is summarized.

3. In Table 8, "Bold m values lie in the ID-guided candidate range [10, 24],
which contains the best accuracy over the full grid" is true for AE and VAE
accuracy, but the VAE text also treats m = 20 as the verification point.
Consider separating "candidate range" from "verification point" in the
caption.

4. The phrase "3x MSE improvement" for the torus in Section 4.2 is
numerically correct using 0.220 to 0.073, but the immediately preceding
sequence also includes 0.138 at m = 5. A short parenthetical such as
"from m = 4 to m = 6" would make the comparison unambiguous.

5. The title and abstract remain dense. This is acceptable for a technical
methods paper, but the abstract could still be shortened by moving some
boundary-case detail to the introduction.
