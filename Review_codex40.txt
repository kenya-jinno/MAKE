Summary
-------
Revision 40 adequately addresses the two remaining minor comments from
Review_codex39.txt. The Section 4 experiment overview now avoids implying a
completed full-protocol Fashion-MNIST validation, and the repository
URL/DOI issue is correctly left as a final submission/editorial matter. I
find no new major methodological problem.

The manuscript is suitable for publication after very minor editorial
corrections. The only substantive remaining issue I found is a local wording
inconsistency about DANCo: Section 2 says DANCo is "left as future work,"
whereas Section 4.3.2 actually uses DANCo as a robustness cross-check.

Assessment of previous review responses
---------------------------------------
Minor comment 1 from Review_codex39, on the experiment-overview wording for
Fashion-MNIST, has been addressed. Section 4 now asks whether "the full
protocol work[s] on MNIST, what evidence is available for Fashion-MNIST,"
which correctly distinguishes the fully validated MNIST case from the
limited Fashion-MNIST evidence. This is consistent with Section 4.3.6:
"Step 1 is thus multi-seed for Fashion-MNIST; the VAE-side AU verification
(Step 3), by contrast, remains a single-seed preliminary observation..."
The possible over-reading noted in the previous review has therefore been
removed.

Minor comment 2, on keeping the production-data statement aligned with the
final repository, is adequately handled for the review stage. Section 4.1
states that the material is attached for review and will be deposited "upon
acceptance" in a public GitHub/Zenodo repository with URL and DOI. The Data
Availability statement similarly says that the repository URL and DOI "will
be added to the final manuscript." This is acceptable at submission/review
stage, assuming the final production version contains the actual links.

Typographical/presentation item 1 from Review_codex39, on MDPI production
placeholders, remains as an expected production matter. The source still has
`\articlenumber{0}`, blank `\datereceived`, `\daterevised`,
`\dateaccepted`, and `\datepublished` fields, commented ORCID placeholders,
and a commented external-editor placeholder. These are not scientific
defects but must be completed or cleaned during final MDPI production.

Typographical/presentation item 2 from Review_codex39, on cross-references,
does not reveal a new problem in the checked source locations. I did not
recompile the manuscript in this read-only review environment, but the
source-level section, table, and appendix references inspected for the
previously disputed points are coherent.

Major comments
--------------
1. No remaining major methodological objection.

The manuscript consistently frames the proposed procedure as a conditional,
diagnostic protocol rather than a general solution. The evidence hierarchy
is now clear: synthetic manifolds and MNIST provide the main validation;
Fashion-MNIST Step 3, strengthened-beta Conv-VAE behavior, and natural-image
plateau observations are explicitly exploratory or unresolved.

Minor comments
--------------
1. Correct the local DANCo wording in Section 2.

Section 2 currently says: "As ID estimators we adopt TwoNN and MLE (DANCo
is more precise but computationally expensive and left as future work)."
However, Section 4.3.2 states that the authors "apply DANCo and ESS" to the
MNIST reference-AE latents, and Table 5 reports DANCo values. Please revise
the Section 2 wording, for example to say that DANCo is not used as the
primary estimator but is used as an auxiliary robustness cross-check on
selected MNIST latents.

2. Ensure that the final Data Availability statement contains the actual
repository URL and DOI.

The present "upon acceptance" wording is acceptable now, but the final
published version should not retain this placeholder language.

Typographical and presentation issues
-------------------------------------
1. MDPI production placeholders remain: `\articlenumber{0}`, blank
`\datereceived`, `\daterevised`, `\dateaccepted`, and `\datepublished`
fields, and commented ORCID placeholders. These should be completed or
removed as appropriate before publication.

2. The revision-history comments at the top of the LaTeX source are harmless
because they do not appear in the compiled manuscript, but the production
source may be cleaner if these internal review notes are removed before
final submission.
