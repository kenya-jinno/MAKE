Overall recommendation: accept

Summary
-------
Revision 41 adequately addresses the remaining substantive correction from
Review_codex40.txt. The Section 2 DANCo wording no longer says that DANCo is
"left as future work"; it now correctly states that DANCo is not a primary
estimator and is used as "an auxiliary robustness cross-check on selected
MNIST latents."

I find no new major methodological problem. The manuscript remains carefully
qualified: the full protocol is validated on synthetic manifolds and MNIST,
Fashion-MNIST Step 3 is explicitly preliminary, and Conv-VAE/natural-image
results are framed as applicability boundaries. The manuscript is suitable
for publication, subject only to expected final production completion of
repository/DOI and MDPI metadata placeholders.
