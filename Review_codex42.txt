Review of main_paper_MAKE42.pdf
Target journal: MDPI Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction (MAKE)
Reviewer: Codex
Date: 2026-07-13

Overall recommendation: accept

Summary
-------
Revision 42 adequately addresses the substantive points raised in
Peer_Review_Report41.md. The manuscript now gives a more explicit and
appropriately qualified account of the natural-image limitations, upgrades
the two previously weak single-seed claims to 3-seed evidence, and expands
the linear-surrogate discussion to include the aggregated-posterior mismatch
KL(q_phi(z)||p(z)) via the ELBO-surgery decomposition. The new text is
internally consistent with the supplied multi-seed experiment logs.

The revised manuscript remains cautious about scope. MNIST is presented as
the clean fully connected validation case; Fashion-MNIST is not overstated
as a full pass, but is correctly treated as a diagnostic lower-V2-bound
boundary case; the high-beta Conv-VAE experiment is presented as V1
recovery without V2 order consistency; and natural-image Step 3 remains an
open problem. This is an appropriate posture for publication.

Assessment of previous review responses
---------------------------------------
Major point 1, on natural-image generalization hypotheses in Sections 5.6.2
and 6.4, has been addressed. The natural-image subsection now gives concrete
hypotheses for why CIFAR-10/SVHN behave differently from MNIST: manifold
entanglement, texture-driven shrinkage of the effective decoder noise
variance, and the mismatch between flat isotropic latents and hierarchical
image factors. The discussion section carries these hypotheses forward into
specific future-work directions, including hierarchical VAEs and
class-/region-wise estimation. The added Sonderby et al. reference is used
for the hierarchical-VAE direction.

Major point 2, on multi-seed upgrades of Fashion-MNIST Step 3 and
Exp-ConvV-HiBeta, has been addressed. The Fashion-MNIST section now reports
Exp-Fashion-MS as a 3-seed, 300-epoch FC-VAE verification. The supplied log
results support the table: at m=20 all three seeds have AU=7, so the
multi-seed mean is AU(20)=7.0 with zero standard deviation; V1 passes
because 7/20=0.35, while the lower V2 bound is violated relative to
hat d_ID=10. The text correctly treats this as an over-pruning/boundary
diagnosis rather than as a protocol success.

The high-beta Conv-VAE upgrade is also adequately handled. The supplied
Exp_ConvV_HiBeta_MS.json log supports the main claims: at beta_max=10 and
m=256 the seed AUs are 93, 103, and 91, giving approximately 95.7 +/- 5.2;
at beta_max=20 and m=256 they are 66, 67, and 71, giving approximately
68.0 +/- 2.2. These values justify the statement that increased beta
restores truncation/V1, but not V2 order consistency with 3*hat d_ID=30.

Major point 3, on the gap between the linear-Gaussian surrogate and
nonlinear VAE behavior, has been addressed. Proposition 2 and the following
remarks now state the rate-distortion approximation more explicitly, and
Appendix A.1 adds the ELBO-surgery identity
E_x KL(q_phi(z|x)||p(z)) = I(x;z) + KL(q_phi(z)||p(z)). The text also
explains the sign of the dropped mismatch term and why it would tend to
amplify the effective KL penalty rather than explain Conv-VAE no-truncation
failures. This is a useful and appropriately bounded clarification; it does
not overclaim a nonlinear theorem.

Minor point 1, on abstract density, has been addressed. The abstract is
still information-dense, but it now foregrounds the closed-loop
estimate-set-verify protocol and removes excessive detail about the MNIST
slowdown region. It also clearly states the conditional nature of the
claim.

Minor point 2, on Figure 2 and Figure 5 legibility, appears addressed at
the source level. The revision history states that the figures were
regenerated with larger fonts and black-and-white-safe line styles, and the
captions remain clear. I found no source-level inconsistency in the figure
references or numbering: the relevant figures are still referred to as the
MNIST multi-seed AU figure and the Exp-ConvV-HiBeta figure.

Minor point 3, on clarifying the 2*hat d_ID buffer in Step 2, has been
addressed. Section 3.2.2 now explicitly states that the multiplier 2 is a
buffer against the unknown gap d_emb - d_ID, not an estimate of d_emb, and
that the window is a starting point rather than a guaranteed upper bound.
This directly addresses the possible confusion between d_ID and d_emb.

Internal consistency checks
---------------------------
The new Fashion-MNIST content is internally consistent. The text, table,
and log agree that AU(20)=7.0 across all three seeds, V1 passes, and the
lower V2 bound fails. The new third row in the failure-mode table covers
"AU saturates below hat d_ID" and points readers to the Fashion-MNIST
diagnosis, which is exactly how the main text uses the result.

The threshold-invariance ranges are stated consistently with the decision
rule. The manuscript reports V1-threshold invariance on the multi-seed mean
for (0.67, 0.94), V2-factor invariance for (2.3, 6.8), per-seed invariance
for V1 in (0.70, 0.93) and V2 factor in (2.3, 6.6), and lower-V2-bound
coefficient invariance in (0.70, 1.0]. These statements are coherent with
the MNIST AU/m boundary at 13.3/20 approximately 0.67 and the
Fashion-MNIST lower-bound ratio 7/10=0.70.

The Conv-VAE high-beta numbers are consistent between the main text,
appendix table, and the supplied JSON log. The paper correctly distinguishes
the beta_max=4 reference failure, the beta_max=10/20 V1 recovery, and the
remaining V2 violation.

The reference list contains 30 entries, including the new Sonderby et al.
and Hoffman & Johnson entries. The new citations are placed where they are
needed: hierarchical latent structure for natural images and ELBO surgery
for the KL(q(z)||p(z)) decomposition. I did not identify an uncited or
obviously misplaced new reference.
