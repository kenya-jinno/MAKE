Overall recommendation: accept with minor corrections

Summary
-------
Revision 39 adequately addresses the four minor comments and four
typographical/presentation items from Review_codex38.txt. The remaining
scientific claims are now consistently qualified: MNIST is the fully
validated real-data case, Fashion-MNIST Step 3 is explicitly preliminary,
single-seed exploratory observations are labeled as such, the MNIST
TwoNN/MLE convention is stated, and the natural-image and threshold
statements have been narrowed.

I find no new major methodological problem. The manuscript is suitable for
publication after minor editorial corrections, mainly MDPI production
placeholders and one residual local wording issue about Fashion-MNIST in
the experiment overview.

Assessment of previous review responses
---------------------------------------
Minor comment 1 from Review_codex38, on the contradictory Fashion-MNIST
sentence, has been addressed. Section 4.3.6 now states: "Step 1 is thus
multi-seed for Fashion-MNIST; the VAE-side AU verification (Step 3), by
contrast, remains a single-seed preliminary observation..." This removes
the previous contradiction.

Minor comment 2, on the global multi-seed sentence in Section 4.1, has
been addressed. Section 4 now says: "All main-text VAE verification results
used for the principal verdicts are multi-seed experiments..." and then

Minor comment 3, on the MNIST search-window convention, has been addressed.

m_ver = 20 from the primary TwoNN estimate and also verifies the

been addressed. Section 1.2 no longer implies that the Step-1 reference-AE

while Step-1 stability is supported by subsampling confidence intervals,
estimator cross-checks, and the 3-seed reference-model comparison.

Typographical/presentation item 2, on the V2 threshold-invariance range,

Typographical/presentation item 3, on the natural-image table caption, has
been addressed. The Table 11 caption now says: "For CIFAR-10, Step 1

Typographical/presentation item 4, on shortening the abstract, has been
sufficiently addressed. The abstract remains dense, but it is more focused
and no longer overstates the boundary-case evidence.

Major comments
--------------
1. No remaining major methodological objection.

The manuscript now consistently frames the protocol as conditional and
diagnostic, and the evidence hierarchy is clear.

Minor comments
--------------
1. Slightly refine the experiment-overview wording for Fashion-MNIST.

Section 4 says the real-data validation asks whether "the full protocol
work[s] on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST." The detailed text later correctly
states that Fashion-MNIST has multi-seed Step 1 only and single-seed
preliminary Step 3. To avoid a last possible over-reading, change this
overview phrase to "does the protocol work on MNIST, and what evidence is
available for Fashion-MNIST..."

2. Keep the production-data statement aligned with the final repository.

The Data Availability section says the repository URL and DOI will be added
"upon acceptance." This is acceptable now, but the final version should
contain the actual GitHub/Zenodo link and DOI.
