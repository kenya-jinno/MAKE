Review of main_paper_MAKE38.pdf
Target journal: MDPI Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction (MAKE)
Reviewer: Codex
Date: 2026-07-09

Overall recommendation: accept with minor corrections

Summary
-------
Revision 38 adequately addresses the remaining substantive issues from
Review_codex37.txt. The protocol is now stated with a pre-registered primary
Step-1 estimator, the torus margin is consistently reported as m >= 5 in the
fine-grid experiment and m = 6 on the coarse grid, the multi-seed claims are
substantially narrowed, and the V1/V2 threshold-sensitivity statement now
distinguishes mean-verdict and per-seed ranges. The manuscript continues to
present the method as a conditional diagnostic workflow rather than as a
general bottleneck-selection algorithm.

I find no remaining major methodological objection. The paper is suitable
for publication after minor corrections, mainly production placeholders and
a few local wording issues that could otherwise confuse readers about which
experiments are multi-seed, which quantities are primary estimates, and
which results are exploratory.

Major comments
--------------
1. No remaining major methodological objection.

The previous major and minor scientific concerns have been resolved or
appropriately reframed. The remaining points below are minor corrections for
clarity, reproducibility presentation, and final MDPI production.

Minor comments
--------------
1. Clarify the sentence on Fashion-MNIST protocol status.

Section 4.3.6 says: "A full multi-seed application of the protocol including
VAE-side AU verification was confirmed only in a single-seed preliminary
observation..." This is internally contradictory: "full multi-seed" cannot
be "only single-seed." The intended meaning appears to be that Fashion-MNIST
has multi-seed Step-1 evidence, while VAE-side AU verification remains a
single-seed preliminary observation. Please rewrite this sentence directly.

2. Slightly refine the global multi-seed sentence in Section 4.1.

Section 4.1 states that "All VAE verification results in the main text are
multi-seed experiments with at least 3 seeds," followed by an exception for
single-seed exploratory observations separated into the appendix. However,
the main text itself briefly reports single-seed high-beta Conv-VAE values
in Section 4.7.1 and a single-seed Fashion-MNIST VAE observation in
Section 4.3.6. These are labeled exploratory, so the evidence is not
misrepresented, but the sentence would be cleaner as "All non-exploratory
VAE verification results in the main text..." or "All main-text VAE
verification results used for the principal verdicts..."

3. Make the MNIST search-window convention explicit in one place.

The manuscript now correctly uses primary TwoNN d_hat_ID = 10 for
m_ver = 20 and treats MLE 11.3--11.9 as a cross-check. Section 4.3.3,
however, still says that from "d_hat_ID approximately 10--12" the AE search
window is [10, 24] and the VAE generous setting is m >= 20. This is
reasonable, but readers may wonder why the upper endpoint uses the
TwoNN--MLE envelope whereas the VAE verification point uses primary TwoNN.
A short parenthetical such as "AE window shown as the TwoNN--MLE envelope;
VAE m_ver follows the primary TwoNN rule" would remove this last ambiguity.

4. Avoid implying that the Step-1 reference-AE route is fully multi-seed in
the MNIST "full protocol" claim.

In Section 1.2, the contribution statement says that multi-seed validation
of the full protocol is given on MNIST. Later, Table 5 states that the main
reference-AE Step-1 sweep is single-seed, with uncertainty quantified by
subsampling and cross-checks, while other Step-1 stability evidence comes
from the 3-seed real-geometry experiment. This is acceptable, but the
claim would be more exact if it said "multi-seed AU verification on MNIST,
with Step-1 stability supported by subsampling, estimator cross-checks, and
the 3-seed reference-model comparison."

Typographical and presentation issues
-------------------------------------
1. MDPI production placeholders remain: article number 0, empty
received/revised/accepted/published fields, commented ORCID placeholders,
and no final repository URL/DOI. These should be completed before final
publication.

2. In Section 3.2.3, the V2 threshold-invariance range is stated as
"(2.3, 6.6)" for both mean and per-seed verdicts. The supporting JSON gives
the mean lower edge as 2.233... and the per-seed lower edge as 2.3. The
current statement is conservative and not wrong, but for exact
reproducibility it would be better to write "approximately (2.24, 6.6) for
the mean and (2.3, 6.6) per seed" or to state that the displayed range is
rounded conservatively.

3. In Section 4.7.2, the natural-image table caption says Step 1 agrees
with Pope et al. "only near m approximately 2 d_hat_ID." This is accurate
for CIFAR-10, but less so for SVHN, where the text emphasizes a larger
discrepancy. Consider changing the caption to "CIFAR-10 agrees..." or
"Step 1 agrees for CIFAR-10..." to avoid overgeneralizing across both
datasets.

4. The abstract is still quite long and densely packed. This is acceptable
for a methods paper at this stage, but if MDPI requests tightening, the
boundary-case details on Conv-VAEs and natural images could be shortened
and left to the introduction and applicability table.
