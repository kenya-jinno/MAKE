                                         Abstract                                                                                                     1


                                         Latent-width selection depends on both diagnostic information and the search policy using                    2

                                         it. We retrospectively compare six policies on stored variational-autoencoder candidates                     3

                                         from four image datasets, five training seeds and five regularisation weights. A validation                  4

                                         anchor defines the quality target, and one canonical timing snapshot prices every request.                   5

                                         The historical intrinsic-dimension window incurs structurally redundant work, so its                         6

                                         expense contrast alone cannot identify the value of dimension information. A revised                         7

                                         local policy and a matched midpoint control expose the trade-off between requests and                        8

                                         the smallest qualifying width. At the inherited defaults, the dimension-initialised policy                   9

                                         matches the full-grid minimum in all 100 conditions, while the midpoint control matches                     10

                                         in 98; the former uses ascending fallback on three datasets. Across 10,000 retrospective                    11

                                         coefficient/threshold settings, 155 local outputs miss the grid minimum despite meeting                     12

                                         quality. Reference-bank acquisition and reuse change the cost interpretation. A new one-                    13

                                         seed MNIST experiment evaluates Monte Carlo ELBO selection, while pruning adaptations                       14

                                         remain separate implementation audits. Active-unit prediction does not establish an                         15

                                         additional benefit on its near-ceiling task. These results support explicit search, quality and             16

                                         cost specifications rather than a universal ranking of dimension-selection methods.                         17




                                         Keywords: variational autoencoder; latent dimension; intrinsic dimension; active units;                     18

                                         model selection; reconstruction quality; reproducibility                                                    19

                                    2. Related Work                                                                                       47


                                    2.1. Dimension, Reconstruction and Latent Activity                                                    48


                                         PCA [9], Isomap [10] and locally linear embedding [11] construct low-dimensional                 49

                                    representations. ID estimators instead quantify local dimension from sample geometry. We              50

                                    use TwoNN and an MLE cross-check; other approaches include DANCo, expected simplex                    51

                                    skewness and geodesic entropic graphs [12–14]. Estimator limitations and dependence                   52

                                    on the sampled distribution motivate cross-checks [15,16]. Graph-based methods are                    53

                                    outside this experiment’s scope; we do not infer that they would resolve or reproduce the             54

                                    observed disagreements. Minimal-spanning-tree isolation forests also use graph geometry               55

                                    for anomaly scoring [17]; that objective is distinct from selecting a VAE width under a               56

                                    reconstruction target. Lower ID in learned representations has also been observed in deep             57

                                    networks [18,19].                                                                                     58

                                         FONDUE [4] searches for the capacity preceding a separation between ID estimates of              59

                                    sampled and posterior-mean representations. Its appendix gives FONDUE-VAR, which                      60

                                    chooses a count from latent-variable types. AU and variable types share an interest in                61

                                    unused coordinates but are different statistics: AU thresholds the across-data variance               62

                                    of posterior means, whereas the type classifier uses moments of posterior variances [6].              63

                                    Agreement of their final counts is not equivalence of their stability or information content.         64

                                         GECO with L0 regularisation optimises a gated VAE under a reconstruction constraint,             65

                                    with KL and gate-count terms [7,20]. The source uses ARM; our adaptation uses hard-                   66

                                    concrete gates [21]. Neither the nonconvex optimisation nor our implementation guarantees             67

                                    a global minimum capacity. Our validation anchor plus tolerance is a particular way of                68

                                    setting an external quality yardstick, not an identical implementation of the source’s                69

                                    constraint.                                                                                           70

                                         ARD-VAE uses a hierarchical relevance prior [8]. The source tunes a regularisation               71

                                    weight, uses a Gaussian approximation for its closed-form KL, and selects axes explaining             72

                                    99% of a Jacobian-weighted variance score. It is therefore not threshold-free. The local              73

                                    adaptation has additional differences, including a finite-step decoder score and a fixed              74

                                    selection-stage weight; these are detailed in Appendix C.2.                                           75

                                    3.1. Models and Quality Target                                                                                 86


                                         We distinguish the ID-reference AE, which supplies a representation for estimation, the                   87

                                    quality anchor, an ordinary VAE at the largest candidate width, and the selected model, the                    88

                                    ordinary VAE evaluated after dimension selection. Write M for the candidate grid, s for a                      89

                                    training seed, and Ds (m, β) for validation per-pixel MSE of the deterministic reconstruction                  90

                                    g(µ( x )). The implemented target is                                                                           91

                                    3.2. Canonical Replay Prices and Historical Ledgers                                                            104


                                         Replay uses one immutable candidate snapshot. A logical key contains dataset, width,                      105

                                    training seed, requested epochs, early-stopping option and β; architecture, likelihood                         106

                                    and split are fixed by the dataset configuration. Each unique requested key, including                         107

                                    the quality anchor, is charged its duration in this snapshot once. Metrics and prices are                      108

                                    therefore identical whenever replay policies request the same key. These are paired uses of                    109

                                    stored records, not independent retraining.                                                                    110

                                         Earlier method ledgers sometimes attach different durations to the same logical key.              111

                                    Even when validation and test summaries agree exactly, missing historical weights and exe-             112

                                    cution identifiers prevent proving checkpoint identity. We therefore do not mix those prices           113

                                    in the new policy comparison. Appendix B.1 preserves the historical account and resolves               114

                                    every anchor/final role to its key, source and both available durations in a supplementary             115

                                    CSV. The quality anchor and its price in each replay come from the same record.                        116

                                         Let Ccand sum the canonical prices of requested candidates and Cref be the nine-AE                117

                                    bank’s recorded training cost. Cold use charges Ccand + Cref for an ID policy; warm use                118

                                    charges Ccand when that bank already exists. A 25-task batch (five seeds by five weights               119

                                    for one dataset) allocates Cref /25 per task. This does not turn shared-bank outcomes into             120

                                    independent ID replications. All accounts exclude missing ID-computation and some                      121

                                    post-training evaluation times. They estimate attributed training costs, not new end-to-end            122

                                    wall-clock measurements. Historical probe and FONDUE calibration costs are reported                    123

                                    separately from the replay policies.                                                                   124



                                    3.3. Outputs and Termination                                                                           125


                                          Each trial records a raw returned dimension, the width of the evaluated model, the               126

                                    ID reliability decision where applicable, fallback use, the logged termination state, the              127

                                    budget flag, quality attainment, and whether the final width is smaller than the anchor.               128

                                    These events can overlap. In particular, fallback can produce a compact model that meets               129

                                    the target. The label SUCCESS in an older log indicates ordinary termination; it is not itself         130

                                    evidence of target attainment.                                                                         131

                                          A budget-exceeded trial contributes its consumed time and, if it has a returned model,           132

                                    that model’s dimension and metrics. A raw dimension below one is reported as degenerate;               133

                                    its time and failure count remain, while MSE is missing because no final model exists. The             134

                                    original implementation checks budgets after completed calls and can still obtain a final              135

                                    model. The reported limits are therefore soft accounting limits, not hard interruptions.               136

                                    Outcome tables preserve these distinctions.                                                            137

                                    3.5. Retrospective Search Policies                                                                     148


                                         We define the following replay on 19 September, after the earlier results and review.             149

                                    A full-grid reference returns m∗ = min{m ∈ M : Ds (m, β) ≤ TD,s }. Ascending search                    150

                                    obtains the anchor and visits increasing widths until the first pass; it identifies m∗ without         151

                                    a monotonic-error assumption. The historical ID procedure exhausts a lower window                      152

                                    when ID is accepted and uses minimum-MSE coarse search otherwise. Its full executed                    153

                                    pseudocode is retained in Appendix C.1.                                                                154

                                         The revised ID-local policy uses ID to choose a starting width rather than an exhaustive               155

                                    window. If the start passes, it walks downwards to the first failure; otherwise it walks                    156

                                    upwards to the first pass. This can omit many candidates, but can miss a smaller qualifying                 157

                                    width if the pass/fail sequence is not monotone. Rejected ID banks now invoke ascending-                    158

                                    retrospective policy.                                                                                       160

                                    4. Theoretical Background                                                                                   176


                                          For an ideal smooth d-manifold, Whitney’s embedding theorem gives an embedding                        177

                                    into R2d [23]. Exact continuous reconstruction through an injective encoder entails an                      178

                                    embedding, but neither a noisy finite-sample ID estimate nor an approximate neural                          179

                                    reconstruction satisfies these assumptions automatically. The multiplier two is therefore                   180

                                    a heuristic under evaluation, not a quality guarantee or a lower bound inferred from                        181

                                    measured ID.                                                                                                182

                                          In linear Gaussian latent-variable models, informative directions depend on the co-                   183

                                    variance spectrum and noise scale; analyses of linear VAEs and posterior collapse make this                 184

                                    relation explicit [9,24]. VAE alignment with principal directions has also been studied [25].               185

                                    These results motivate measuring how regularisation affects coordinate use. They do not                     186

                                    equate AU with intrinsic or minimum embedding dimension in a nonlinear VAE. AU also                         187

                                    depends on the latent coordinates and the threshold used to count them.                                     188

                                    5.2. Methods and Readouts                                                                           230


                                          Grid search selects by validation MSE, the ELBO proxy, or linear-probe validation             231

                                    accuracy. Probes use posterior means, multinomial logistic regression, up to 2000 iterations,       232

                                    and C ∈ {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10} chosen by validation. Labels are digit/apparel/ CIFAR classes            233

                                    and the three dSprites shapes. Both capacity and probe regularisation use validation labels         234

                                    in the probe selector. No centring, scaling or whitening is applied to the probe latents;           235

                                    logistic regression uses its default intercept. Coarse search uses Algorithm A1’s subset; the       236

                                    fixed baseline uses width 32.                                                                       237

                                          FONDUE is a local PyTorch implementation using TwoNN and a fixed ID-gap thresh-               238

                                    old 0.2d0 . It searches integer widths, which can lie outside the ordinary candidate grid.          239

                                    Its selected width is trained exactly. At β = 1, short training uses one epoch for MNIST            240

                                    and dSprites and two for Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10. dSprites follows the source’s                  241

                                    epoch choice; elsewhere a seed-42 calibration increases epochs until two consecutive pre-           242

                                    dictions agree, stopping at five. Equality is first tested after runs at one and two epochs;        243

                                    if predictions at e − 1 and e agree, the returned epoch count is e − 1, and both calibration        244

                                    costs remain charged. Other β-specific choices are in Appendix C. FONDUE+Q maps the                 245

                                    returned width upwards onto the grid, obtains the anchor, and extends upwards until                 246

                                    quality is met or its budget flag is set. This adds an external reconstruction rule to FONDUE.      247

                                          FONDUE-VAR starts at round(2d0 ) and doubles width until the declared type condi-             248

                                    tion is met. With mixed variables kept it returns active plus mixed once a passive variable         249

                                    appears; otherwise it returns the active count once a mixed or passive variable appears.            250

                                    We implement the description-based classifier: posterior variance is exponentiated once             251

                                    from log variance. A coordinate is passive if its across-data variance is below 0.1 and its         252

                                    mean is in [0.9, 1.1], active if that variance is below 0.1 and its mean is at most 0.1, and        253

                                    mixed otherwise. The audited public-code version is identified in Appendix C.2; this is not         254

                                    claimed to reproduce every path of that code or its published experimental settings.                255

                                    6.8. Implementation Audit and Degenerate Outputs                                                     480


                                          Two scale inconsistencies were identified in local implementations. First, the earlier         481

                                    VAE loss averaged KL over latent dimensions while summing reconstruction over input                  482

                                    dimensions. Algebraically this gives β effective = β nominal /m. The image results in this paper     483

                                    come from the corrected sum-over-latents convention. The algebra does not imply that                 484

                                    complete optimisation trajectories or AU counts are reproduced simply by relabelling β;              485

                                    the earlier “within 1.7 units” claim is not used here.                                               486

                                          Second, an intermediate GECO adaptation combined per-pixel-mean constraint values              487

                                    with other terms on incompatible scales. The corrected implementation uses summed                    488

                                    squared error and multiplies the per-pixel target by the input dimension. Either sum or              489

                                    mean conventions can be valid if the objective, target and multiplier scales are consistent.         490

                                    The observed behaviour of this hard-concrete adaptation is not attributed to the original            491

                                    ARM implementation.                                                                                  492

                                          FONDUE-VAR’s active-only setting returning zero is a separate phenomenon, not a                493

                                    third sum/mean defect. At MNIST’s one-epoch setting it returns zero on all five trials; no           494

                                    final VAE exists for these outputs. Table 14 shows both settings and their quality/validity          495

                                    7. Discussion                                                                                      497


                                         Search design changes what a cost comparison can identify. Exhausting a lower ID              498

                                    window includes work already sufficient for ascending search; its higher expense is largely        499

                                    determined by construction. The new replay instead exposes the trade-off between fewer             500

                                    candidate requests and certifying the smallest qualifying width. A no-ID local control can         501

                                    also save requests. The results therefore do not attribute every reduction from full-grid          502

                                    search to ID information.                                                                          503

                                         ID-local matches the grid minimum at the inherited default throughout the recorded            504

                                    ladder, but relies on fallback in three datasets. The factorial analysis shows both decision       505

                                    changes at reliability thresholds and nonminimal outputs at other settings. Shared ref-            506

                                    erence banks, one split, retrospective policy design and a small dataset collection limit          507

                                    generalization. Acquisition and reuse costs answer different deployment questions and              508

                                    cannot be collapsed into one universal ranking.                                                    509

                                         Regularisation ranges and AU-threshold responses are different measurements. Some             510

                                    large dimension ranges reflect fallback, grid scale or a changing quality target. A constant       511

                                    output can be fixed by design or by an unvaried implementation argument. Thus the                  512

                                    evidence supports method- and dataset-dependent responses, not a universal robustness              513

                                    ranking. The archived positive AU-versus-baseline result is not robust to preserving logistic-     514

                                    score rankings instead of rounded probabilities. The corrected predictive comparison does          515

                                    not establish an AU-specific advantage over the matched random-feature controls.                   516

                                         The pruning evidence remains deliberately outside the main comparison. Normalizing            517

                                    the stored ARD perturbations corrects one identifiable readout error, but does not validate        518

                                    the prior-update procedure or recover an infinitesimal Jacobian. The native HC trajectory          519

                                    supplies one new implementation diagnostic, not broad source-method validation. The                520

                                    new MC ELBO comparison likewise covers one MNIST seed and proxy-selected check-                    521

                                    points; it does not complete a four-dataset, five-seed ELBO study. Exact ARM/source-ARD            522

                                    reproduction, an independent reference-bank replication, missing natural-image training            523

                                    curves and complete end-to-end timing remain open. The contribution is thus a repro-               524

                                    ducible comparison of specified search policies and diagnostic assumptions, not a definitive       525

                                    ranking of established pruning methods.                                                            526

                                         The synthetic experiments in Appendix D illustrate scale dependence and the effect of         527

                                    zeroing low-variance coordinates. Their counts are observations under a particular loss and        528

                                    coordinate system. They do not show that topology predicts the number of active axes, and          529

                                    their preprocessing prevents treating them as clean estimates of held-out generalisation.          530




                                    8. Conclusions                                                                                     531


                                         We separated latent-width search decisions from diagnostic readouts and implementa-           532

                                    tion history. The historical fixed-window expense contrast does not by itself test the value       533

                                    of ID information. Retrospective local-search and matched-control replays quantify candi-          534




                                                                                                https://doi.org/10.3390/make8010000
Version September 19, 2026 submitted to Mach. Learn. Knowl. Extr.                                                                  21 of 34




                                    date savings, reference acquisition costs and gaps from the full-grid minimum. Reliability                535

                                    thresholds alter which datasets use ID, and some local policies miss qualifying smaller                   536

                                    widths. The paired AU analysis does not establish an AU-specific benefit on its near-ceiling              537

                                    prediction task. A bounded new ELBO experiment and corrected relevance audit improve                      538

                                    measurement validity while leaving source-method validation incomplete. Claims about                      539

                                    efficient capacity selection therefore depend on explicit search rules, quality targets, cost             540

                                    units and implementation evidence.                                                                        541

                                    Algorithm A1 (Historical fixed-window ID procedure (audit)).
                                    Input: splits, M, seed s, β, 10% target margin, floor coefficient 0.001, input TwoNN d0 , reference

                                          audit step, not a constraint used by the coarse selector; its training is charged.

                                    3.    If either check fails, flag ID-unreliable and evaluate the coarse subset. Its zero-based indices are
                                          the unique values of round(geomspace(1, |M|, 4)) − 1. Return its minimum-validation-MSE

                                    4.    Otherwise set m0 = min{m ∈ M : m ≥ 2dˆ}, or m a if the set is empty. Initialise the evaluated set
