                                         Abstract                                                                                                     1


                                         Selecting a variational autoencoder’s latent width requires distinguishing representation                    2

                                         diagnostics from reconstruction requirements. We compare intrinsic-dimension guidance,                       3

                                         ascending and grid searches, FONDUE variants, and local pruning adaptations on four                          4

                                         image datasets with five candidate training seeds. A validation anchor defines an explicit                   5

                                         reconstruction target; final ordinary VAEs provide a common dimension-transfer compari-                      6

                                         son. We report test error, target attainment, termination outcomes and recorded training                     7

                                         costs, with missing overhead identified. At the primary MNIST setting, ID guidance and                       8

                                         ascending search select the same six-dimensional model, with training accounts of 715 and                    9

                                         182 seconds. Shared reference-bank checks reject ID guidance on Fashion-MNIST, dSprites                     10

                                         and CIFAR-10, which therefore use a coarse-search fallback. Selected dimensions vary with                   11

                                         regularisation context, but method orderings reverse across datasets. The ARD adapta-                       12

                                         tion did not vary its selection-stage weight and cannot support a zero-sensitivity claim.                   13

                                         Active-unit counts depend on their threshold, and a retrospective paired random-feature                     14

                                         comparison does not establish an AU-specific predictive benefit. These results delimit what                 15

                                         ID and activity diagnostics alone establish about capacity under the tested architectures,                  16

                                         quality targets and implementation choices.                                                                 17

                                    2. Related Work                                                                                       49


                                    2.1. Dimension, Reconstruction and Latent Activity                                                    50


                                         PCA [9], Isomap [10] and locally linear embedding [11] construct low-dimensional                 51

                                    representations. ID estimators instead quantify local dimension from sample geometry. We              52

                                    use TwoNN and an MLE cross-check; other approaches include DANCo, expected simplex                    53

                                    skewness and geodesic entropic graphs [12–14]. Estimator limitations and dependence                   54

                                    on the sampled distribution motivate cross-checks [15,16]. Graph-based methods are                    55

                                    outside this experiment’s scope; we do not infer that they would resolve or reproduce the             56

                                    observed disagreements. Lower ID in learned representations has also been observed in                 57

                                    deep networks [17,18].                                                                                58

                                         FONDUE [4] searches for the capacity preceding a separation between ID estimates of              59

                                    sampled and posterior-mean representations. Its appendix gives FONDUE-VAR, which                      60

                                    chooses a count from latent-variable types. AU and variable types share an interest in                61

                                    unused coordinates but are different statistics: AU thresholds the across-data variance               62

                                    of posterior means, whereas the type classifier uses moments of posterior variances [6].              63

                                    Agreement of their final counts is not equivalence of their stability or information content.         64

                                         GECO with L0 regularisation optimises a gated VAE under a reconstruction constraint,             65

                                    with KL and gate-count terms [7,19]. The source uses ARM; our adaptation uses hard-                   66

                                    concrete gates [20]. Neither the nonconvex optimisation nor our implementation guarantees             67

                                    a global minimum capacity. Our validation anchor plus tolerance is a particular way of                68

                                    setting an external quality yardstick, not an identical implementation of the source’s                69

                                    constraint.                                                                                           70

                                         ARD-VAE uses a hierarchical relevance prior [8]. The source tunes a regularisation               71

                                    weight, uses a Gaussian approximation for its closed-form KL, and selects axes explaining             72

                                    99% of a Jacobian-weighted variance score. It is therefore not threshold-free. The local              73

                                    adaptation has additional differences, including a finite-step decoder score and a fixed              74

                                    selection-stage weight; these are detailed in Appendix B.1.                                           75

                                    3.1. Models and Quality Target                                                                                 86

                                    ordinary VAE evaluated after dimension selection. Write M for the candidate grid, s for a                      89

                                    g(µ( x )). The implemented target is                                                                           91

                                    3.2. Cost Accounting and Shared Candidates                                                                     104


                                         Candidates with the same dataset, architecture, likelihood, width, seed, epoch bud-                       105

                                    get, stopping option and β are shared through a cache. Each selection rule is charged                          106

                                    the recorded training duration of every distinct candidate it requests. Reusing a candi-                       107

                                    date within the rule costs no additional training time. Consequently, identical selected                       108

                                    candidates have identical metrics across methods. They are paired observations, not inde-                      109




                                                                                                          https://doi.org/10.3390/make8010000
Version September 18, 2026 submitted to Mach. Learn. Knowl. Extr.                                                              4 of 26




                                    pendently retrained method-specific models. A selected candidate already trained during              110

                                    search is reused; a short-search candidate receives the ordinary final training schedule.            111

                                         The original ledgers did not charge the quality anchor to every method. For this                112

                                    revision, its recorded duration is added wherever absent; if it is also the final ordinary           113

                                    VAE, it is counted once. The resulting Ctrain includes candidate training, ID-reference              114

                                    AE training, the anchor, and separately recorded probe time where used for selection.                115

                                    Training timers include epoch-wise validation, but omit several post-training evaluations            116

                                    and ID computations. The pruning timers have slightly different boundaries, including                117

                                    their final readout. Thus Ctrain is an attributed recorded-time account, not an end-to-end           118

                                    wall-clock measurement. Exact overhead cannot be recovered from these logs. FONDUE                   119

                                    epoch-calibration time is reported separately because it is performed once per dataset–β             120

                                    condition. No complete wall-clock speedup is claimed.                                                121



                                    3.3. Outputs and Termination                                                                         122


                                          Each trial records a raw returned dimension, the width of the evaluated model, the             123

                                    ID reliability decision where applicable, fallback use, the logged termination state, the            124

                                    budget flag, quality attainment, and whether the final width is smaller than the anchor.             125

                                    These events can overlap. In particular, fallback can produce a compact model that meets             126

                                    the target. The label SUCCESS in an older log indicates ordinary termination; it is not itself       127

                                    evidence of target attainment.                                                                       128

                                          A budget-exceeded trial contributes its consumed time and, if it has a returned model,         129

                                    that model’s dimension and metrics. A raw dimension below one is reported as degenerate;             130

                                    its time and failure count remain, while MSE is missing because no final model exists. The           131

                                    original implementation checks budgets after completed calls and can still obtain a final            132

                                    model. The reported limits are therefore soft accounting limits, not hard interruptions.             133

                                    Outcome tables preserve these distinctions.                                                          134

                                    4. Theoretical Background                                                                                    150


                                          For an ideal smooth d-manifold, Whitney’s embedding theorem gives an embedding                         151

                                    into R2d [22]. Exact continuous reconstruction through an injective encoder entails an                       152

                                    embedding, but neither a noisy finite-sample ID estimate nor an approximate neural                           153

                                    reconstruction satisfies these assumptions automatically. The multiplier two is therefore                    154

                                    a heuristic under evaluation, not a quality guarantee or a lower bound inferred from                         155

                                    measured ID.                                                                                                 156

                                          In linear Gaussian latent-variable models, informative directions depend on the co-                    157

                                    variance spectrum and noise scale; analyses of linear VAEs and posterior collapse make this                  158

                                    relation explicit [9,23]. VAE alignment with principal directions has also been studied [24].                159

                                    These results motivate measuring how regularisation affects coordinate use. They do not                      160

                                    equate AU with intrinsic or minimum embedding dimension in a nonlinear VAE. AU also                          161

                                    depends on the latent coordinates and the threshold used to count them.                                      162

                                    6.6. Implementation Audit and Degenerate Outputs                                                     379


                                          Two scale inconsistencies were identified in local implementations. First, the earlier         380

                                    VAE loss averaged KL over latent dimensions while summing reconstruction over input                  381

                                    dimensions. Algebraically this gives β effective = β nominal /m. The image results in this paper     382

                                    come from the corrected sum-over-latents convention. The algebra does not imply that                 383

                                    complete optimisation trajectories or AU counts are reproduced simply by relabelling β;              384

                                    the earlier “within 1.7 units” claim is not used here.                                               385

                                          Second, an intermediate GECO adaptation combined per-pixel-mean constraint values              386

                                    with other terms on incompatible scales. The corrected implementation uses summed                    387

                                    squared error and multiplies the per-pixel target by the input dimension. Either sum or              388

                                    mean conventions can be valid if the objective, target and multiplier scales are consistent.         389

                                    The observed behaviour of this hard-concrete adaptation is not attributed to the original            390

                                    ARM implementation.                                                                                  391

                                    third sum/mean defect. At MNIST’s one-epoch setting it returns zero on all five trials; no           393

                                    7. Discussion                                                                                       396


                                         The most direct evidence is the paired MNIST comparison: under the tested model                397

                                    family, grid and target, the implemented ID-guided selector gives no recorded-training-cost         398

                                    advantage over ascending search. Its reliability checks reject the other three datasets, but        399

                                    those repeated rejections share a reference bank. They indicate failure of this applicability       400

                                    check under these representations, not that ID methods generally cannot work on those               401

                                    datasets. Different reference architectures, grids, targets or reliable estimators could change     402

                                    the comparison.                                                                                     403

                                         Regularisation ranges and AU-threshold responses are different measurements. Some              404

                                    large dimension ranges reflect fallback, grid scale or a changing quality target. A constant        405

                                    output can be fixed by design or by an unvaried implementation argument. Thus the                   406

                                    evidence supports method- and dataset-dependent responses, not a universal robustness               407

                                    ranking. The archived positive AU-versus-baseline result is not robust to preserving logistic-      408

                                    score rankings instead of rounded probabilities. The corrected predictive comparison does           409

                                    not establish an AU-specific advantage over the matched random-feature controls.                    410

                                         Several limits remain material. The pruning rows evaluate dimension transfer through           411

                                    local adaptations, including nearest-grid rounding; their native model performance and              412

                                    exact-source implementations are not compared. Complete ID/evaluation timings are                   413

                                    missing, so a strict end-to-end cost ranking cannot be reconstructed. The ID selector’s             414

                                    expansion branch is ineffective. A fixed-quality β intervention, a correctly varied ARD             415

                                    selection weight with source-matched relevance scoring, and an exact ARM comparison                 416

                                    require new training and are not claimed as completed experiments. The single split and             417

                                    shared references also limit uncertainty estimates. These restrictions are preferable to            418

                                    interpreting the current records as broader evidence than they provide.                             419

                                         The synthetic experiments in Appendix C illustrate scale dependence and the effect of          420

                                    zeroing low-variance coordinates. Their counts are observations under a particular loss and         421

                                    coordinate system. They do not show that topology predicts the number of active axes, and           422

                                    their preprocessing prevents treating them as clean estimates of held-out generalisation.           423




                                    8. Conclusions                                                                                      424


                                         We compared latent-width selection rules using common candidate-model families,                425

                                    explicit validation targets and a documented training-time account. At the primary MNIST            426

                                    setting, ID guidance and ascending search select the same six-dimensional VAE, while                427

                                    ID guidance incurs more recorded training time. ID estimates depend on representation               428

                                    and sample size; AU counts depend on their readout threshold. The paired predictive                 429

                                    comparison does not establish an AU-specific advantage over the evaluated random-feature            430

                                    controls. Dimension variation across regularisation contexts differs by method and dataset,         431

                                    without a transferable ranking. These findings delimit what ID and AU alone can establish           432

                                    about the capacity needed for a chosen reconstruction quality under the tested conditions.          433
