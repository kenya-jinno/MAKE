1. What prompted the request

The reviewers raised substantive concerns about the rigour of our
validation, about the absence of a comparison with existing dimension-
selection methods, and about the heuristic character of our thresholds. In
responding, we carried out a complete audit of our implementation rather
than only editing the text. That audit found a defect in our
variational-autoencoder objective: the Kullback-Leibler term was averaged
over the latent dimensions as well as over the batch, while the
reconstruction term was summed over the input dimensions. The effective
regularisation weight was therefore beta/m rather than beta, where m is
the very quantity our study varies. The same convention was present in all
twelve of our VAE experiment scripts.

This defect is consequential. Under the corrected objective, the number of
active units no longer depends on m; we were able to reproduce our
previously reported curve to within +/-1.7 units simply by re-reading it as
a function of beta/m. Every VAE experiment in the manuscript therefore had
to be re-run.

2. What we have already completed

We have re-run all experiments under the corrected objective, with a
protocol fixed in advance (quality criterion, budget, termination states
and decision rules) and with every subsequent change recorded in a change
log. Across four datasets and five independent seeds per condition, we
have completed: a like-for-like comparison against FONDUE (Algorithm 1)
and FONDUE-VAR (Algorithm 3), reimplemented from the published
pseudocode; full grid search under reconstruction, ELBO and downstream
criteria; coarse search and fixed-dimension baselines; and controls that
isolate the contribution of each component of our procedure.

3. Why the extension is needed

These results do not support several of the central claims of the
submitted manuscript. In particular, intrinsic-dimension guidance conferred
no measurable advantage: a plain ascending scan under the same quality
criterion reached the same or a better outcome at lower cost on every
dataset, and the guidance step declined to apply on two of the three image
datasets. We consider it our responsibility to report this rather than to
retain the original claims, and we are therefore reframing the
contribution from a proposed selection method to an empirical study of
which diagnostics survive scrutiny and which do not. Rewriting the
manuscript on that basis, and removing every statement that the new
evidence no longer supports, is not something we can complete responsibly
within the remaining week.

The extension would also allow us to address Reviewer 2's comparison
concern properly. We estimate that implementing and evaluating the two
remaining comparison methods (a GECO/L0-style pruning method and ARD-VAE)
requires approximately thirteen hours of GPU time on our hardware, plus
implementation, and we would prefer to include them rather than to declare
them as a gap.

We wish to be explicit that we will not present unfinished experiments as
completed, and we will not defer the unresolved points to future work
without saying so. If the requested period is not available, we will
submit within the current deadline with the reduced scope stated plainly
in the response letter.

We are grateful to the reviewers, whose comments led directly to the audit
that uncovered the defect described above. We would be glad to provide any
further detail that would help you assess this request.
